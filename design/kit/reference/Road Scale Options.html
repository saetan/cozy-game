Road scale options for the 2 m game grid

Same village in each option: 3 houses (2×2, 3×2, 2×2), market (2×2), 6 farm plots, garden paths, a straight, bend, T, crossing and an asphalt ↔ dirt join. Same ground (40 × 32 m) and the same camera, so land use reads directly. Faint lines = 2 m game cells.

Recommendation: C — 6 m streets in the centre, 2 m dirt lanes outward

  1. Keep the 6 m kit as the "Street" (village centre). It reads instantly as a town, has sidewalks for residents and room for two cars. Paint it on the 3×3 snap only.
  2. Add 2 m lanes as the cell-paintable tool — dirt lane first (farms, orchards, outskirts), asphalt lane optional for quiet cul-de-sacs. They fit the grid and the painting UX exactly.
  3. Joins are automatic: a lane painted against a street edge gets a lane mouth and the street drops its kerb (same mechanism as driveways). Allow only one lane per 2 m edge cell.
  4. Don't use 2 m asphalt for the whole village (B): it's compact and paintable, but a single lane with no sidewalk reads as a driveway, cars can't pass, and dead ends need a turning cell.
A

6 m streets (kit as is)

At game distance

  • Most "town"-like: two lanes, centre line, kerbs, sidewalks, zebra crossings. Very readable.
  • Streets dominate the frame — roughly ⅓ of the village is road and sidewalk; houses look small beside them.

Awkward

  • Painting snaps to 3×3 blocks: one tap covers 9 cells; you can't nudge a road by one cell.
  • Lots must fit the 3-cell rhythm between streets — the 3×2 house fills its block exactly, so there's no garden room.
  • Driveways and paths need the dropped-kerb hand-off from connectLot; works, but every lot must face a street edge.
  • The dirt road is also 6 m — heavy for a farm track.
B

2 m lanes (new set)

At game distance

  • Cosiest and most compact — feels like a hamlet. Houses become the hero.
  • The kit car is ~1.3 m wide (1.4 m over fenders) on 1.7 m asphalt — it fits, but the lane reads like a driveway, not a road. A 1.6–1.8 m car would touch the kerbs.

Awkward

  • Single lane: two vehicles can't pass; they need passing/waiting rules or a 2-cell-wide painted section.
  • Dead ends have no room to turn — the car spins on the spot here. Needs a turning patch or reversing.
  • No sidewalk: residents share the lane or need parallel paths (extra cells).
  • Asphalt lanes and driveways are nearly the same width and colour family — hard to tell apart.
C

Mixed: 6 m centre + 2 m dirt lanes

At game distance

  • Clear hierarchy: busy paved centre, soft dirt lanes to the fields — matches the gameplay (cars fast on roads, slower on dirt).
  • Farm side stays light and green; the lane is the same scale as the plots and paths.

Awkward

  • A lane meets the street at one of the 3 edge cells of a tile, so it can look off-centre; the middle cell is the tidy default.
  • The lane crosses the sidewalk on a dropped kerb with a dirt spill — reads fine, but residents and vehicles share that cell.
  • Lanes can't join a street tile's corner or a junction arm (only flat sidewalk edges).

New 2 m pieces (B and C) — additions in kit/lanes.js, 6 m pieces unchanged

Row 1Asphalt lane: dead end · straight (car) · corner · T · cross · join → dirt Row 2Dirt lane: dead end · straight (bike) · corner · T · cross Row 3Lane mouth: dirt lane meets a 6 m street; the street drops its kerb

Profile

  • Asphalt lane: 1.7 m asphalt (#bcb5bb) + 0.15 m low kerb each side (h 0.10, #d6cfc4) = 2.0 m. Top ASPH_H 0.04. Dead end gets a white stop line.
  • Dirt lane: 1.5 m dirt (#dfc7a3), ruts ±0.38 m, ragged chunks and tufts to ~1.8 m. Top DIRT_H 0.03.
  • Corners are quarter-annuli centred on the cell corner (centre-line r 1 m), like the 6 m bends.

API

  • laneTile({ conn, seed }), dirtLaneTile({ conn, seed }) — conn[N|E|S|W] = 'road' | 'dirt' | 'street'. A 'dirt' side on asphalt = join (kerb runs on, dirt spill); 'street' = lane mouth (flared to 2 m).
  • laneMouthSpill() — dirt on the street's dropped sidewalk, pivot on the shared edge.
  • buildLanes(cells, { streets, ox, oz, seed }) → { items, cuts }; items match buildRoads (key, make, x, y, z, ry); pass cuts to buildRoads to drop the street kerb.
  • Cell pivot at ground centre, 1 unit = 1 m, named meshes (lane_asphalt, lane_kerb, dirt_rut…) reusing the road materials.
